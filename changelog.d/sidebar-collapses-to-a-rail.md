### Changed
- Collapsing the sidebar on a desktop (the header's toggle, ⌘⇧B, or `sidebar=hidden`) now minimizes it to a rail
  instead of hiding it: the column stays at a rail's width with one mark a row — the agent, branch or terminal
  glyph that leads each workspace and agent, its state at the row's corner and its tooltip still saying what it
  opens — while the names and actions it has no room for wait for the sidebar.
