### Added
- A quick reply can ask before it acts: with the shield on in **Settings → Chat → Quick replies**,
  the first tap only arms it (the chip takes the danger tint) and the second sends, or the arm
  lapses after five seconds. `commit and push` asks by default.
- **Put replies in the message box** makes a tap add the reply at the caret instead of sending it,
  so a reply can be edited or extended first. Off by default: one tap still sends.
