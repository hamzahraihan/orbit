### Added
- **Send later**: a clock beside Attach lines the box's text up for a moment instead of now — in
  15 minutes, in an hour, tomorrow at 9, or a time you pick. The message is kept on the PC that
  owns the pane, so it is typed there even with every browser closed, and a chip above the input
  card shows it until then with the X that takes it back. A moment that arrives while the agent's
  own input box holds a draft is left alone: the chip waits for that box to clear, looks again every
  minute, and says why if it never did. A send that failed keeps its chip and says why, so text you
  wrote is never dropped, never typed over and never sent twice. A PC whose bridge predates this
  shows no clock at all, rather than a control that only refuses.
